# VOLUME X: FUTURE UPDATES

## Overview
MASS AWAKENING is designed as a living, evolving game that can grow and expand over years of development and community involvement. This volume outlines the systems and philosophies that will guide future expansions, ensuring that the game remains coherent, balanced, and exciting while incorporating new ideas from players and developers. The focus is on sustainable growth that respects the core vision while allowing for meaningful innovation.

## Expansion Philosophy
- **Core Vision Preservation**: Expansions should enhance, not contradict, the fundamental vision of MASS AWAKENING
- **Coherence and Consistency**: New content should fit logically within the existing universe
- **Player Agency**: Expansions should empower meaningful player choice and expression
- **Technical Sustainability**: New systems should be maintainable and performant
- **Community Involvement**: Players should have meaningful ways to contribute to the game's evolution
- **Backward Compatibility**: Existing worlds and saves should remain functional
- **Forward Compatibility**: Systems should be designed to accommodate future growth
- **Quality Over Quantity**: Meaningful, well-integrated content is preferred to superficial additions
- **Accessibility**: New content should be accessible to players of different abilities and playstyles
- **Monetization Ethics**: If applicable, monetization should be fair and not pay-to-win
- **Preservation of Player Investment**: Existing progress and creations should retain value
